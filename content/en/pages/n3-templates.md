# Templates for the Your First 90 Days course

<p class="lead">The Your First 90 Days course (N3) has 7 lessons, for new employees and people in their first 0–2 years of work. Each lesson completes one sheet of your <b>90-day notebook</b>.</p>

## How to take the course

- **Suggested schedule:** one lesson a week, 15–20 minutes of reading, then do the "Real-world application" at work.
- **Spaced review:** after each lesson, answer the Self-check again after 1, 3 and 7 days, without rereading the lesson first.
- **Use real sheets:** the sheets are everyday tools, not homework to hand in. Update Sheet 1 every week.
- **Trainers and team leaders:** you can use the course to support new staff. Lesson 7 fits an end-of-probation review.
- **Confidentiality:** do not write customer names or confidential company figures on printouts you take outside. Follow your workplace's rules.
- **Privacy:** the site collects no data. Your notebook lives on paper or on your own device.
