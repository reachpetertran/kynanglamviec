# Templates for the Senior and Specialist course

<p class="lead">The Senior and Specialist course (N4) has 8 lessons, for experienced staff, senior specialists and experts. Each lesson completes one sheet of your <b>specialist toolkit</b>.</p>

## How to take the course

- **Suggested schedule:** one lesson a week, 20 minutes of reading, then do the "Real-world application" with a real piece of work.
- **Spaced review:** after each lesson, answer the Self-check again after 1, 3 and 7 days, without rereading the lesson first.
- **Use real sheets:** the sheets are working tools. Show them to colleagues and let them correct them, especially Sheets 1, 3 and 6.
- **Learn in pairs:** two seniors can take the course together and swap sheets to peer-check. Lessons 4 and 5 work well as pair practice.
- **Legal:** Lesson 7 teaches how to read regulations. It is not legal advice. For important matters, ask your legal team or a lawyer.
- **Confidentiality:** do not write client names or confidential company figures on printouts you take outside. Follow your workplace's rules.
- **Privacy:** the site collects no data. Your toolkit lives on paper or on your own device.
