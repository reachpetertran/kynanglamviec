## 1. Objectives

- Divide your first 90 days into **3 stages**, each with one focus.
- Write **3 measurable goals** and agree them with your manager.

## 3. Explanation

The first months in a new job bring a lot to learn. Without a plan, daily tasks carry you along. At the end, it is hard to show what you have learned. Many workplaces also have a probation period at this time. The 90-day mark here is only a suggested way to divide the time, not a legal deadline.

**Divide 90 days into 3 stages:**

| Stage | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | **Learn** | Understand the work, processes, tools and people. Ask a lot, take notes. |
| Days 31–60 | **Do it yourself** | Do the main tasks of the role yourself, with few reminders. |
| Days 61–90 | **Contribute** | Work steadily, and propose one small improvement. |

**Write measurable goals.** "Understand the job well" is hard to measure. "Handle 10 return requests without asking" can be measured. Each stage should have a goal like that.

**Agree it with your manager.** In your first week, ask your manager for 20 minutes. Ask: "After 90 days, what would you like me to be able to do?" Then share your plan for their comments. That way, you both have the same expectations.

<span>Ask expectations</span>

**Review every 30 days.** At the end of each stage, assess yourself: was the goal met, and why? Send your manager a short update using the [progress report](../k2-write-a-progress-report/) format. It is also a good time to [ask for feedback](../k7-ask-for-and-use-feedback/).

**Do not forget people.** Note who is responsible for what. Knowing who to ask helps you learn much faster.

## 4. Workplace example

> **Hypothetical scenario.** Sara has just started as a purchasing assistant at a furniture manufacturer. In her first week, she meets her department head to ask about expectations.

The head hopes that after 3 months, Sara can place orders with regular suppliers on her own. Sara writes her plan:

- **Days 1–30 (Learn):** learn the 6-step ordering process; know the names and products of the 15 main suppliers. *Measure:* draw the process from memory, and the head confirms it is right.
- **Days 31–60 (Do it yourself):** prepare 20 purchase orders herself, checked by the head before sending. *Measure:* at most 2 orders need correcting.
- **Days 61–90 (Contribute):** propose a way to shorten order approval time. *Measure:* a written proposal reviewed by the head.

The head suggests adding how to check wood quality on delivery. Sara adds it to stage 1.

**Why this works:** the goals are measurable and agreed with her manager from the start.

## 5. Practice

> **Hypothetical scenario.** You have just started as a receptionist at a mid-range hotel. After 3 months, the manager wants you to handle check-in and check-out on your own. You also need to handle common guest requests.

Write a 3-stage plan with one measurable goal per stage.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

What do you need to learn in stage 1? For example: the booking system, the check-in process, common guest requests.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Measurable goals usually have a number: how many times, how many errors, how long.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Model answer</summary>

- **Days 1–30 (Learn):** watch and assist colleagues with 30 check-ins and check-outs. List the 10 most common guest requests and how to handle them.
- **Days 31–60 (Do it yourself):** handle check-in and check-out alone on shift, with at most 1 error a week.
- **Days 61–90 (Contribute):** write sample answers for the 10 common requests, for new staff to use.

(The numbers are hypothetical.)
</details>

## 7. Reflection

1. At your last job or place of study, could you show what you had learned after the first 3 months?
2. Have you ever asked a manager about their expectations? If not, what held you back?

## 8. Real-world application

If you are new in a job: in the next 7 days, book 20 minutes with your manager to ask about 90-day expectations, then write 3 goals. If you have been there a while: write 3 goals for the next 90 days in your current role.
