K7 Self-directed learning and career development

Explore careers, teach yourself new tasks and plan your own development path.

Cluster III. Self-management · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Autonomy and self-learning

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can name many jobs and what people in those jobs do.
  • I can ask adults about their work.
  • I can say what skills a job needs.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K7.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can research a field before choosing a course or internship.
  • I can teach myself a new task using documents, videos and people who know.
  • I can write down my strengths and what I still need to learn.
  • Know yourself and choose directions to test with three circles K7 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Read part-time and internship job ads to choose well and avoid traps K7 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Learn a new task fast by watching, trying, writing your own process notes and testing yourself K7 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Review: Peer-check your Job-ready file and make a 30-day plan K7 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can set learning goals for my first 90 days in a new job.
  • I can ask for feedback and use it to improve.
  • I can record evidence of good work to use in reviews.
  • Ask for feedback and turn it into action K7 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 15 min ✓ Done
  • Plan your learning for your first 90 days in a new job K7 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Review: prepare for your end-of-probation or 90-day review K7 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can make a 1–3 year career plan with measurable milestones.
  • I can move into a new role using skills I can take with me.
  • I can build a professional network to learn from.
  • Review the course: renew your career with a 1–3 year plan K7 · Level 4 Proficient · N4 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can develop myself as a leader based on feedback from many directions.
  • I can help people in my team plan their career paths.
  • I can set regular time aside for my own and my team's learning.
  • From specialist to manager K7 · Level 5 Leading · N5 · 20 min ✓ Done
