K2 Speaking and writing clearly

Speak and write briefly and to the point, so the reader understands and knows what to do.

Cluster I. Communication · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Communication and collaboration

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can briefly introduce myself or a piece of work.
  • I can write a work message with a greeting and clear content.
  • I can speak loudly and clearly to a small group.
  • Communicate in your first week at work by greeting, asking and writing complete messages K2 · Level 1 Exploring · N2 · 15 min ✓ Done

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can write an email with a clear subject, one purpose and one specific request.
  • I can write a one-page CV and answer interview questions with real examples.
  • I can present something I have done in three minutes.
  • Write a work email with one purpose and one clear request K2 · Level 2 Starting · N2, N3 · 15 min ✓ Done
  • Tell a real example with STAR in interviews K2 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Write a one-page CV when you have no work experience yet K2 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Prepare your first interview in three stages, before, during and after K2 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can report progress: what is done, what is blocked, what help I need.
  • I can adjust how I speak to suit the listener, such as a customer or a manager.
  • I can write meeting notes with decisions, owners and deadlines.
  • Write a short progress report in 3 parts K2 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 15 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can present a proposal with data, options and risks.
  • I can negotiate an agreement that all sides can accept.
  • I can write a guide that others can follow on their own.

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can communicate direction so the whole team understands why and what to do.
  • I can help others write and present better.
  • I can share difficult news with the team clearly and respectfully.
