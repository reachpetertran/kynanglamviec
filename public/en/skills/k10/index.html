K10 Digital skills, AI and workplace rules

Use digital tools and AI safely; understand contracts, safety and confidentiality at work.

Cluster V. Digital workplace · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: (Informatics)

Human rights and social compliance are covered on trachnhiemxahoi.com (Vietnamese). Here we learn the practical knowledge you need at work. Go to trachnhiemxahoi.com

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can name the technology a familiar job uses.
  • I can keep my password secret.
  • I can say why I should not share personal information online.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K10.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can use email, spreadsheets and work chat at a basic level.
  • I can read the main terms of a contract or internship agreement before signing.
  • I can recognise common scam emails and messages.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K10.

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can use AI to support my work and check the output myself.
  • I can follow my workplace's data protection and safety rules.
  • I can organise and name files so others can find them.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K10.

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can automate a repetitive task with a digital tool.
  • I can assess the risk before putting data into an AI tool.
  • I can show colleagues how to use digital tools safely.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K10.

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can set rules for using AI and data in my team.
  • I can choose digital tools for the team based on need, cost and risk.
  • I can handle a data leak according to my organisation's procedure.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K10.
