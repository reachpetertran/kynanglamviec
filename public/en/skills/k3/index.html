K3 Problem solving and decision making

Define the real problem, find the cause, compare options and choose a course of action.

Cluster II. Thinking · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Problem solving and creativity

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can say clearly where I am stuck.
  • I can ask the right person for help at the right time.
  • I can follow existing steps when I face an unfamiliar task.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K3.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can follow existing steps to fix a familiar problem.
  • I can think of at least two solutions before asking my manager.
  • I can describe a problem: what happens, when, and what I have tried.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K3.

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can find the root cause, not just treat the symptom.
  • I can compare options against clear criteria and then choose.
  • I can use data to test an idea about the cause.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K3.

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can see a problem within the whole system and spot knock-on effects.
  • I can decide with incomplete information and state what I am assuming.
  • I can assess risks and prepare a backup plan for a decision.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K3.

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can make strategic decisions that weigh long-term risks.
  • I can build ways of working so the team solves problems on its own.
  • I can set shared decision criteria that the team applies consistently.
