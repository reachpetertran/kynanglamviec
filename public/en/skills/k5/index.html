K5 Planning and keeping work commitments

Take on tasks clearly, write them down, prioritise, meet deadlines and warn early when you may be late.

Cluster III. Self-management · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Autonomy and self-learning

Responsibility as a personal quality is covered on daynentang.com. Here we learn the skill side: writing tasks down, tracking them, warning early. Go to daynentang.com

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can take on a task and say clearly when it will be done.
  • I can finish my part of a group task.
  • I can prepare everything I need before starting a task.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K5.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can write every task I am given into one list with deadlines.
  • I can warn early when I see I may miss a deadline.
  • I can split a big task into small steps with their own deadlines.
  • Track the tasks you are given and flag delays early K5 · Level 2 Starting · N2, N3 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can estimate time and set priorities when many tasks arrive at once.
  • I can renegotiate a deadline or scope when there is not enough time.
  • I can keep a task-tracking system going for many weeks.
  • Prioritise competing tasks and renegotiate deadlines K5 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • The numbers that keep you alive: cash flow, break-even, margin K5 · Level 3 Secure · N7 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can plan a small project with milestones, owners and risks.
  • I can track several people's progress and adjust in time.
  • I can manage work that depends on other people or teams.
  • Run a lean project on one page K5 · Level 4 Proficient · N4 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can allocate people and resources according to my unit's priorities.
  • I can build processes so the team keeps commitments without reminders.
  • I can state clearly what the team will not do, to stay focused on priorities.
  • Review the course: allocate people by your team's priorities K5 · Level 5 Leading · N5 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Turn strategy into work for each division K5 · Level 5 Leading · N6 · 20 min ✓ Done
