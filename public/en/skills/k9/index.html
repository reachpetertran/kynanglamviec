K9 Leadership and developing others

Lead shared work, delegate, give feedback and help others improve.

Cluster IV. Collaboration and leadership · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: –

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can show a newcomer a task I already know.
  • I can take a small role in a group, such as timekeeper or note-taker.
  • I can help my group stay on track during a task.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K9.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can suggest a way forward for the group when no one speaks up.
  • I can lead by example by working on time and to standard.
  • I can take responsibility for coordinating a small group task.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K9.

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can guide a newcomer as they settle into the job.
  • I can give colleagues specific, behaviour-based feedback.
  • I can run a short meeting that ends on time with conclusions.
  • Hire and keep your first employees K9 · Level 3 Secure · N7 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can mentor someone over several months.
  • I can influence decisions without a formal title.
  • I can lead a project team without line-management authority.
  • Mentor a colleague with a 3-month plan and four rungs of handover K9 · Level 4 Proficient · N4 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can delegate clearly, follow up and give regular feedback.
  • I can identify and develop successors.
  • I can set goals for the team and link them to the organisation's goals.
  • Delegate clearly, follow up and give regular feedback K9 · Level 5 Leading · N5 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • From managing a team to leading an organisation K9 · Level 5 Leading · N6 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Review the course: prepare future leaders and succession K9 · Level 5 Leading · N6 · 20 min ✓ Done
