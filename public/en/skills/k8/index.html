K8 Teamwork and respecting differences

Share out work, coordinate and work well with people of different ages, backgrounds and views.

Cluster IV. Collaboration and leadership · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Communication and collaboration

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can do my part in a group task.
  • I can listen when a teammate shares an idea.
  • I can share materials and information with my group.
  • Finishing a task together K8 · Level 1 Exploring · N1 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can share out work with my group and remind each other of deadlines.
  • I can work with people of different ages, regions and ways of thinking.
  • I can ask for help and say thank you when I get it.
  • Split work in a group project with a task board K8 · Level 2 Starting · N2 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can support an overloaded colleague and still finish my own work.
  • I can disagree respectfully, focusing on the work.
  • I can share information in time so colleagues can carry on.
  • Work with colleagues in other teams and disagree respectfully K8 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can mediate conflict between team members.
  • I can coordinate with other departments towards a shared goal.
  • I can work well with people of other languages and cultures, or remotely.
  • Listen to challenge and resolve professional disagreements in a team K8 · Level 4 Proficient · N4 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Handle conflict and difficult conversations in a team K8 · Level 4 Proficient · N5 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can build a team with clear roles where everyone can contribute.
  • I can set working rules that suit people in different circumstances.
  • I can review and improve how the team works together on a regular basis.
  • Build a team with clear roles where everyone can contribute K8 · Level 5 Leading · N5 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Design decision rights between divisions K8 · Level 5 Leading · N6 · 20 min ✓ Done
