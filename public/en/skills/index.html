10 work skills

Each skill has 5 levels, described with "I can…" statements. Use them to assess yourself and choose lessons.

ClusterSkillWhat it covers
I. CommunicationK1 Listening and understandingListen fully, ask about what is unclear and confirm, so you do what the other person actually needs.
I. CommunicationK2 Speaking and writing clearlySpeak and write briefly and to the point, so the reader understands and knows what to do.
II. ThinkingK3 Problem solving and decision makingDefine the real problem, find the cause, compare options and choose a course of action.
II. ThinkingK4 Creativity and improvementThink of new ways of working, test small, measure the result, then roll out.
III. Self-managementK5 Planning and keeping work commitmentsTake on tasks clearly, write them down, prioritise, meet deadlines and warn early when you may be late.
III. Self-managementK6 Adaptability and persistenceKeep doing good work when plans, tools or the environment change.
III. Self-managementK7 Self-directed learning and career developmentExplore careers, teach yourself new tasks and plan your own development path.
IV. Collaboration and leadershipK8 Teamwork and respecting differencesShare out work, coordinate and work well with people of different ages, backgrounds and views.
IV. Collaboration and leadershipK9 Leadership and developing othersLead shared work, delegate, give feedback and help others improve.
V. Digital workplaceK10 Digital skills, AI and workplace rulesUse digital tools and AI safely; understand contracts, safety and confidentiality at work.
