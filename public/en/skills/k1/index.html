K1 Listening and understanding

Listen fully, ask about what is unclear and confirm, so you do what the other person actually needs.

Cluster I. Communication · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Communication and collaboration

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can listen to a short instruction without interrupting.
  • I can repeat a simple instruction in my own words.
  • I can follow an instruction with two or three steps.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K1.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can ask when the expected result, deadline or quality standard is unclear.
  • I can note the main points when I receive a task or attend a meeting.
  • I can take a work phone call and write down names, numbers and times correctly.
  • Take instructions and confirm them before you start K1 · Level 2 Starting · N2, N3 · 15 min ✓ Done

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can summarise what someone said and ask them to confirm before I act.
  • I can notice the concern behind what a customer or colleague says.
  • I can hear a customer's complaint out before I explain.
  • Listen in meetings and confirm actions with a summary message K1 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 15 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can listen to opposing views without rushing to argue back.
  • I can ask open questions to clarify what each side really needs.
  • I can notice when people use the same word but mean different things.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K1.

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can listen in a coaching way, helping others find their own solution.
  • I can run meetings where people dare to speak openly.
  • I can receive and act on critical feedback about how I manage the team.
  • Listen to coach in one-to-one meetings K1 · Level 5 Leading · N5 · 20 min ✓ Done
  • Hear weak signals and help bad news travel up K1 · Level 5 Leading · N6 · 20 min ✓ Done
