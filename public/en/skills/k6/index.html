K6 Adaptability and persistence

Keep doing good work when plans, tools or the environment change.

Cluster III. Self-management · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Autonomy and self-learning

The inner side (meditation, Stoicism) is on tamhonkhoe.com. Here we learn how to keep working when things change. Go to tamhonkhoe.com

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can keep working when a plan changes slightly.
  • I can say when I feel confused by a new task.
  • I can try again after my first attempt does not work.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K6.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can get used to a new tool or process quickly.
  • I can take feedback without abandoning my work.
  • I can ask why when a rule or plan changes.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K6.

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can keep my work quality up under pressure or when plans change.
  • I can learn from a mistake and do things differently.
  • I can learn new skills on my own when the job requires it.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K6.

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can help colleagues adapt when a process changes.
  • I can prepare a backup plan for important work.
  • I can keep on schedule while running old and new work at the same time.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K6.

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can lead a team through a major change, explaining the reasons and the roadmap.
  • I can keep the team's workload at a level it can sustain.
  • I can listen to and address the team's concerns during change.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K6.
