K4 Creativity and improvement

Think of new ways of working, test small, measure the result, then roll out.

Cluster II. Thinking · Vietnam's 2018 school curriculum, core competency: Problem solving and creativity

The 5 levels of this skill

Level 1 Exploring

You know what the skill is and can use it with step-by-step guidance.

  • I can offer a new idea for a group task.
  • I can notice a task that could be done faster or more simply.
  • I can ask "is there another way?" while doing a task.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K4.

Level 2 Starting

You can use it on your own in familiar tasks, sometimes with a reminder.

  • I can try a small new way of working and look at the result.
  • I can write down an improvement idea with the reason for it.
  • I can learn a colleague's work tip and try it.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K4.

Level 3 Secure

You use it reliably in many situations, including under pressure.

  • I can propose an improvement covering the current state, the solution and the benefit.
  • I can run a small trial before rolling out widely.
  • I can measure results before and after a small improvement.
  • Write a small improvement proposal with the current situation, solution and benefits K4 · Level 3 Secure · N3 · 20 min ✓ Done

Level 4 Proficient

You handle complex or new situations and can support colleagues.

  • I can borrow ideas from other fields to solve my team's problem.
  • I can lead an improvement project from idea to measured result.
  • I can persuade the people involved to try a new way of working.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K4.

Level 5 Leading

You build ways of working for the whole team and help others grow this skill.

  • I can create an environment where people dare to propose and experiment.
  • I can choose which ideas get investment based on evidence.
  • I can set aside specific time and resources for the team's experiments.

No English lesson at this level yet. Vietnamese lessons for K4.
