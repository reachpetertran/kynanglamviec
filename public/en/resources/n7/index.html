Templates for the Founder and Start-up course

The Founder and Start-up course (N7) has 8 lessons, for founders, small business owners and early-stage start-ups. Each lesson completes one sheet of your founder's handbook.

How to take the course

  • Suggested schedule: one lesson a week, 20 minutes of reading, then do the "Real-world application" with your real business.
  • Spaced review: after each lesson, answer the Self-check again after 1, 3 and 7 days, without rereading the lesson first.
  • The course thread: Sheets 1 and 2 give you evidence. Sheet 4 gives you numbers. Sheet 7 uses both in your pitch.
  • Level of evidence: each lesson ends with a box saying whether its tools rest on research or are this site's proposal.
  • Limits of the course: Lessons 5, 6 and 8 are not legal advice; their examples come from Vietnam. Lesson 4 is not accounting or tax advice. Legal sources show the date checked.
  • Privacy: the site collects no data. Your handbook lives on paper or on your own device.
