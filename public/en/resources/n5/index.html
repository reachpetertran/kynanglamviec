Templates for the Manager and Team Leader course

The Manager and Team Leader course (N5) has 8 lessons, for new and experienced managers and team leaders. Each lesson completes one sheet of your manager's handbook.

How to take the course

  • Suggested schedule: one lesson a week, 20 minutes of reading, then do the "Real-world application" with your real team.
  • Spaced review: after each lesson, answer the Self-check again after 1, 3 and 7 days, without rereading the lesson first.
  • Use the sheets with your team: Sheets 2, 3 and 6 are done with the team. Sheets 4 and 5 are tools for private conversations.
  • Learn in pairs: two managers can take the course together and practise Lessons 4 and 5 in pairs, one playing the employee.
  • Limits of the course: Lesson 5 does not replace a disciplinary process. Lesson 7 is not legal advice. For important matters, ask HR or your legal team.
  • Confidentiality: Sheets 4 and 5 hold information about other people. Store them carefully, not on a shared desk.
  • Privacy: the site collects no data. Your handbook lives on paper or on your own device.
