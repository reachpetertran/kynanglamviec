Templates for the Getting Ready for Work course

The Getting Ready for Work course (N2) has 7 lessons, for upper-secondary students, university students, part-time workers, interns and apprentices. Each lesson completes one sheet of your Job-ready file.

How to take the course

  • Suggested schedule: one lesson a week, 15–20 minutes of reading, then do the "Real-world application" during the week.
  • Spaced review: after each lesson, answer the Self-check again after 1, 3 and 7 days, without rereading the lesson first.
  • Study with a friend: the course works better with a study partner. Lesson 7 needs someone for the peer check.
  • Teachers and careers advisers: you can use the lessons in careers sessions or club meetings.
  • Privacy: the site collects no data. Your file lives on paper or on your own device.
