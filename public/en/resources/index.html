Evidence journal template (STAR)

STAR is a way to tell a work example in 4 parts. It is very useful in interviews.

S – SituationWhere, when, what was the context?
T – TaskWhat did you need to do? What result was expected?
A – ActionWhat exactly did you do?
R – ResultWhat happened? Any numbers? What did you learn?
