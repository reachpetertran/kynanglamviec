Templates for the Senior Leadership course

The Senior Leadership course (N6) has 8 lessons, for division directors, functional directors and leadership teams. Each lesson completes one sheet of your executive handbook.

How to take the course

  • Suggested schedule: one lesson a week, 20 minutes of reading, then do the "Real-world application" with your real organisation.
  • Spaced review: after each lesson, answer the Self-check again after 1, 3 and 7 days, without rereading the lesson first.
  • Work with your leadership team: Sheets 2, 3, 5 and 7 are best done with division heads. Do Sheets 1 and 8 alone first, then discuss.
  • Level of evidence: each lesson ends with a box saying whether its tools rest on research, a capability framework, or this site's proposal.
  • Limits of the course: Lesson 7 is not legal advice. The thresholds in Lesson 5 must follow your charter and internal rules.
  • Confidentiality: Sheets 7 and 8 hold sensitive information about risks and people. Keep them as confidential company documents.
  • Privacy: the site collects no data. Your handbook lives on paper or on your own device.
