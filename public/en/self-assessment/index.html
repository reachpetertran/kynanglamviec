Assess your skills

For each "I can…" statement, choose the option that fits you best. There are no right or wrong answers.

Your answers are saved only in this browser on this device (localStorage). The site sends no data anywhere. Not for children to use alone.

How the suggested level is worked out: the highest level where every statement from Level 1 up to that level is at "Usually" or above. This only helps you choose lessons. It is not a certificate. Answers are shared with the Vietnamese version of this page.

K1 Listening and understanding
L1 I can listen to a short instruction without interrupting.
L1 I can repeat a simple instruction in my own words.
L1 I can follow an instruction with two or three steps.
L2 I can ask when the expected result, deadline or quality standard is unclear.
L2 I can note the main points when I receive a task or attend a meeting.
L2 I can take a work phone call and write down names, numbers and times correctly.
L3 I can summarise what someone said and ask them to confirm before I act.
L3 I can notice the concern behind what a customer or colleague says.
L3 I can hear a customer's complaint out before I explain.
L4 I can listen to opposing views without rushing to argue back.
L4 I can ask open questions to clarify what each side really needs.
L4 I can notice when people use the same word but mean different things.
L5 I can listen in a coaching way, helping others find their own solution.
L5 I can run meetings where people dare to speak openly.
L5 I can receive and act on critical feedback about how I manage the team.

K2 Speaking and writing clearly
L1 I can briefly introduce myself or a piece of work.
L1 I can write a work message with a greeting and clear content.
L1 I can speak loudly and clearly to a small group.
L2 I can write an email with a clear subject, one purpose and one specific request.
L2 I can write a one-page CV and answer interview questions with real examples.
L2 I can present something I have done in three minutes.
L3 I can report progress: what is done, what is blocked, what help I need.
L3 I can adjust how I speak to suit the listener, such as a customer or a manager.
L3 I can write meeting notes with decisions, owners and deadlines.
L4 I can present a proposal with data, options and risks.
L4 I can negotiate an agreement that all sides can accept.
L4 I can write a guide that others can follow on their own.
L5 I can communicate direction so the whole team understands why and what to do.
L5 I can help others write and present better.
L5 I can share difficult news with the team clearly and respectfully.

K3 Problem solving and decision making
L1 I can say clearly where I am stuck.
L1 I can ask the right person for help at the right time.
L1 I can follow existing steps when I face an unfamiliar task.
L2 I can follow existing steps to fix a familiar problem.
L2 I can think of at least two solutions before asking my manager.
L2 I can describe a problem: what happens, when, and what I have tried.
L3 I can find the root cause, not just treat the symptom.
L3 I can compare options against clear criteria and then choose.
L3 I can use data to test an idea about the cause.
L4 I can see a problem within the whole system and spot knock-on effects.
L4 I can decide with incomplete information and state what I am assuming.
L4 I can assess risks and prepare a backup plan for a decision.
L5 I can make strategic decisions that weigh long-term risks.
L5 I can build ways of working so the team solves problems on its own.
L5 I can set shared decision criteria that the team applies consistently.

See skill K3 and its lessons

K4 Creativity and improvement
L1 I can offer a new idea for a group task.
L1 I can notice a task that could be done faster or more simply.
L1 I can ask "is there another way?" while doing a task.
L2 I can try a small new way of working and look at the result.
L2 I can write down an improvement idea with the reason for it.
L2 I can learn a colleague's work tip and try it.
L3 I can propose an improvement covering the current state, the solution and the benefit.
L3 I can run a small trial before rolling out widely.
L3 I can measure results before and after a small improvement.
L4 I can borrow ideas from other fields to solve my team's problem.
L4 I can lead an improvement project from idea to measured result.
L4 I can persuade the people involved to try a new way of working.
L5 I can create an environment where people dare to propose and experiment.
L5 I can choose which ideas get investment based on evidence.
L5 I can set aside specific time and resources for the team's experiments.

K5 Planning and keeping work commitments
L1 I can take on a task and say clearly when it will be done.
L1 I can finish my part of a group task.
L1 I can prepare everything I need before starting a task.
L2 I can write every task I am given into one list with deadlines.
L2 I can warn early when I see I may miss a deadline.
L2 I can split a big task into small steps with their own deadlines.
L3 I can estimate time and set priorities when many tasks arrive at once.
L3 I can renegotiate a deadline or scope when there is not enough time.
L3 I can keep a task-tracking system going for many weeks.
L4 I can plan a small project with milestones, owners and risks.
L4 I can track several people's progress and adjust in time.
L4 I can manage work that depends on other people or teams.
L5 I can allocate people and resources according to my unit's priorities.
L5 I can build processes so the team keeps commitments without reminders.
L5 I can state clearly what the team will not do, to stay focused on priorities.

L1 I can say when I feel confused by a new task.
L1 I can try again after my first attempt does not work.
L2 I can get used to a new tool or process quickly.
L2 I can take feedback without abandoning my work.
L2 I can ask why when a rule or plan changes.
L3 I can keep my work quality up under pressure or when plans change.
L3 I can learn from a mistake and do things differently.
L3 I can learn new skills on my own when the job requires it.
L4 I can help colleagues adapt when a process changes.
L4 I can prepare a backup plan for important work.
L4 I can keep on schedule while running old and new work at the same time.
L5 I can lead a team through a major change, explaining the reasons and the roadmap.
L5 I can keep the team's workload at a level it can sustain.
L5 I can listen to and address the team's concerns during change.

L4 I can build a professional network to learn from.

L5 I can set regular time aside for my own and my team's learning.

L1 I can listen when a teammate shares an idea.

L2 I can work with people of different ages, regions and ways of thinking.
L2 I can ask for help and say thank you when I get it.
