N2 Upper-secondary students, university students, part-timers, interns

You are about to enter your first workplace. Focus: knowing yourself to choose a direction, writing a CV and interviewing, knowing your rights and safety in part-time work, communicating and working in a team.

For: Upper-secondary students, university students not yet working, part-time workers, interns, apprentices.
Focus levels: Levels 1–2

Map of the Getting Ready for Work course (N2)

The course has 7 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: your Job-ready file, with 6 sheets. Each lesson completes one sheet. The last lesson peer-checks the whole file.

  1. Lesson 1. Know yourself, choose a directionDraw a three-circle map, pick 2 directions and plan one small test within 14 days.K7 · Level 2 · 20 min · Upper-secondary, university
  2. Lesson 2. Reading job adsSeparate required and preferred criteria, score your fit, and spot 3 signs to stop.K7 · Level 2 · 20 min · Upper-secondary, university
  3. Lesson 3. One-page CVWrite a 5-part CV, each line as verb + task + result, matched to one specific ad.K2 · Level 2 · 20 min · Upper-secondary, university
  4. Lesson 4. Your first interviewPractise a 60-second introduction, prepare 2 questions to ask, and send thanks within 24 hours.K2 · Level 2 · 20 min · Upper-secondary, university
  5. Lesson 5. Your first weekAsk at the right time with a question log, write 4-part work messages, and report absence or lateness properly.K2 · Level 1 · 15 min · Upper-secondary, university
  6. Lesson 6. Learning new tasks fastUse a 4-step loop and a 1–3–7 day review schedule to remember a new process without asking again.K7 · Level 2 · 20 min · Upper-secondary, university
  7. Lesson 7. Job-ready file (review)Peer-check all 6 sheets with a friend, fix your 3 weakest points and make a 30-day plan.K7 · Level 2 · 20 min · Upper-secondary, university

Coming later: a lesson on rights and safety in part-time work and internships. Labour rules differ by country, so it will cite the law where it applies.
