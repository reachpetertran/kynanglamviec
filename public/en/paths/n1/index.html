Paths › N1

N1 Primary and lower-secondary pupils

At this age the aim is to widen what children know about jobs, not to choose one. Children learn four things: exploring jobs around them, seeing that anyone can do any job, working in a group, and keeping commitments in a task. There is no career-choice quiz.

For: Parents or teachers use it with the child. Children do not use a device alone.
Focus levels: Level 1

The site collects no data about children. Habits, values and character are taught on daynentang.com.

Map of the Exploring Jobs course (N1)

The course has 7 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: the Job card. Lesson 1 makes the cards, each later lesson adds to them or uses them, and the last lesson puts them all on display.

  1. Lesson 1. Jobs around youGo "job hunting" around your home and school, and make your first Job card.K7 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 6–15
  2. Lesson 2. Jobs need skillsBe a "skills detective" and fill in box 5 of your Job card.K7 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 6–15
  3. Lesson 3. Anyone can do itSpot a gender stereotype about jobs and turn it into a question about skills.K7 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 6–15
  4. Lesson 4. Working as a groupMake a "Who – What – Done by when" Task board and create a jobs poster together.K8 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 6–15
  5. Lesson 5. Keep your promiseListen fully, repeat it back, write the deadline; if you are running late, say so early and say what you need.K5 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 11–15 (ages 9–11 with an adult)
  6. Lesson 6. Job interviewPrepare 5 questions, interview someone safely, and tell others about the job in 2 minutes.K7 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 11–15 (ages 9–11 with an adult)
  7. Lesson 7. Job fair (review)Review the whole course with a Job fair: display Job cards, talk about jobs, role-play, and cross-check all 6 lessons.K7 · Level 1 · 20 min · Ages 6–15

Start Lesson 1 Guide and templates for adults
