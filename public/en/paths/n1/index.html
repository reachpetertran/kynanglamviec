Paths › N1

N1 Primary and lower-secondary pupils

At this age the aim is to widen what children know about jobs, not to choose one. Children learn four things: exploring jobs around them, seeing that anyone can do any job, working in a group, and keeping commitments in a task. There is no career-choice quiz.

For: Parents or teachers use it with the child. Children do not use a device alone.
Focus levels: Level 1

The site collects no data about children. Habits, values and character are taught on daynentang.com.

The English course for this group is not ready yet. The full Vietnamese course is here: N1 (Tiếng Việt).

For parents and teachers. N1 lessons are written for an adult to lead with the child. There is no career-choice quiz. The site collects no data about children. The journal and self-assessment are not for children to use alone.

Suggested modules

  1. Jobs around me
  2. Every job needs skills
  3. Anyone can do this job
  4. Finishing a task together
  5. Taking on a task and keeping promises (ages 11–15)
  6. Interviewing someone about their job (ages 11–15)
