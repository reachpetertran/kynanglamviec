N5 Managers and team leaders

You are responsible for other people's results. Focus: moving from specialist to manager, delegating and giving feedback, coaching, handling conflict, building a team and leading change, including bringing AI into the team.

For: Team leaders, first-line and middle managers.
Focus levels: Levels 4–5 (K9 starts from Levels 2–3)

Map of the Manager and Team Leader course (N5)

The course has 8 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: your manager's handbook, with 8 sheets you use with a real team. The last lesson reviews the whole course and builds a resource plan for next quarter.

  1. Lesson 1. From specialist to managerChange how you measure success, choose what to let go, keep and start, and ask for feedback from all sides.K7 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  2. Lesson 2. Making team goals clearWrite a one-page team goal linked to the organisation's goal, then check that the team has understood.K2 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  3. Lesson 3. Delegating and giving feedbackDelegate with a 6-box sheet, choose a level of autonomy, follow up at checkpoints and give timely feedback.K9 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  4. Lesson 4. Listening to coachMake the one-to-one the employee's meeting, ask in 4 steps, listen more than you talk, and know when to instruct.K1 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  5. Lesson 5. Difficult conversationsPrepare with facts, open briefly, hear the other side, agree together and set a review date.K8 · Level 4 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  6. Lesson 6. Building an inclusive teamWrite a team working agreement, share invisible tasks fairly, run meetings where everyone speaks, and review each quarter.K8 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  7. Lesson 7. Leading change and AI adoptionExplain why, test small, hear concerns, support people through the switch, and set team rules for using AI.K6 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders
  8. Lesson 8. Allocating resourcesMixed review of the whole course, compare the work with the team's real capacity, choose what not to do and tell your manager.K5 · Level 5 · 20 min · Managers and team leaders

Note: Lesson 5 teaches conversation skills. It does not replace your organisation's disciplinary process. Lesson 7 is not legal advice; its legal examples come from Vietnam, with the date they were checked. Take first: Mentor a colleague from the N4 course.
