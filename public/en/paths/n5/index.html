N5 Managers and team leaders

You are responsible for other people's results. Focus: moving from specialist to manager, delegating and giving feedback, coaching, handling conflict, building a team and leading change, including bringing AI into the team.

For: Team leaders, first-line and middle managers.
Focus levels: Levels 4–5 (K9 starts from Levels 2–3)

The English course for this group is not ready yet. The full Vietnamese course is here: N5 (Tiếng Việt).

Suggested modules

  1. From specialist to manager
  2. Delegation and feedback
  3. Coaching and developing people
  4. Handling conflict and difficult conversations
  5. Building a team and an inclusive culture
  6. Leading change, including bringing AI into the team
