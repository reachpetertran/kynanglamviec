N4 Senior staff and specialists

You are good at your specialist work. Next step: systems thinking, influence without a title, mentoring colleagues, lean project management and renewing your career.

For: Experienced staff, senior specialists, experts.
Focus levels: Levels 3–4

Map of the Senior and Specialist course (N4)

The course has 8 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: your specialist toolkit, with 8 sheets you use on real work. The last lesson reviews the whole course and builds a 1–3 year career plan.

  1. Lesson 1. Seeing problems as a systemDraw an impact map, ask "and then what?" twice, and find the small change with a lasting effect.K3 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  2. Lesson 2. Deciding with incomplete informationClassify the decision, write down assumptions, set change-your-mind signals and a fallback.K3 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  3. Lesson 3. Persuading without authorityMap the stakeholders, write a one-page proposal, and negotiate on interests rather than positions.K2 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  4. Lesson 4. Resolving professional disagreementsRestate the other person's view until they nod, separate facts from interpretations, then agree on criteria.K8 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  5. Lesson 5. Mentoring a colleagueAgree measurable goals, hand work over in four rungs, and ask before you tell.K9 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  6. Lesson 6. Running a lean projectOne page with goal, scope, milestones, roles, dependencies and risks, plus a 15-minute weekly update.K5 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  7. Lesson 7. Turning rules into actionRead the first and last parts first, separate three dates, and turn each clause into a task with an owner and evidence.K10 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists
  8. Lesson 8. Renewing your careerMixed review of the whole course, 5 transferable skills with evidence, and a small career experiment.K7 · Level 4 · 20 min · Senior staff and specialists

Note: Lesson 7 teaches how to read and apply regulations. It is not legal advice. Its legal examples come from Vietnam, with the date they were checked.
