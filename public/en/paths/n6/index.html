N6 Senior managers and executives

You no longer lead one team but a whole system: many layers of managers, many divisions, many risks. Focus: deciding strategy as a group, turning strategy into action, communicating across layers, designing decision rights, helping bad news travel up, governing risk and AI, and preparing successors.

For: Division heads, functional directors, executive teams, managers of managers.
Focus levels: Levels 4–5

Map of the Senior Leadership course (N6)

The course has 8 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: your executive handbook, with 8 sheets you use with a real organisation. The last lesson reviews the whole course and builds a succession map.

  1. Lesson 1. Leading an organisationSeparate work your direct reports can do from work only an executive can do, then protect time for it.K9 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  2. Lesson 2. Strategic decisionsEstimate from similar projects, then have the whole group imagine failure before you commit.K3 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  3. Lesson 3. Strategy into executionThree priorities at most, each division writes its contribution and dependencies, and a monthly review decides by criteria set in advance.K5 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  4. Lesson 4. Communicating across levelsWrite a one-page direction note, brief middle managers first, then ask the front line whether the message is still right.K2 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  5. Lesson 5. Designing decision rightsFor each common decision, record who proposes, who is consulted, who decides and who is informed. Only one person decides.K8 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  6. Lesson 6. Hearing weak signalsRespond well to bad news, set up a channel that gets key news to the leadership team within 48 hours, and go and listen to the front line yourself.K1 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  7. Lesson 7. Risk, compliance and AI governanceKeep a register of the 10 biggest risks with owners and warning thresholds, know a manager's duties, and list the AI the organisation uses.K10 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives
  8. Lesson 8. Developing successorsMixed review of the whole course, then a succession map for key roles and a development plan for each successor.K9 · Level 5 · 20 min · Senior managers and executives

Note: Lesson 7 is not legal advice; its legal examples come from Vietnam, with the date they were checked. Each tool states its level of evidence: research, recommended practice, or this site's proposal. Take first: the Manager and Team Leader course (N5).
