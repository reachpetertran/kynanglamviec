N7 Founders and start-ups

You are turning an idea into a real business. Focus: customer discovery, experiments with criteria, initiative and bouncing back from failure, managing cash flow, getting the legal basics right when starting, hiring your first people and telling your story to raise resources.

For: Founders, small business owners, household businesses growing into companies, early-stage start-ups.
Focus levels: Levels 2–4 depending on the skill

The N3–N6 lessons on problem solving, delegation and decisions are also useful for founders.

Map of the Founder and Start-up course (N7)

The course has 8 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: your founder's handbook, with 8 sheets you use with a real business. The evidence on Sheets 1–2 and the numbers on Sheet 4 are reused in your pitch on Sheet 7.

  1. Lesson 1. Discovering customersTurn your idea into 3 testable hypotheses, then interview 10 customers about what they have done, not whether they like your idea.K1 · Level 4 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  2. Lesson 2. Experiments and pivotsChoose the riskiest hypothesis, design a cheap test with a metric and thresholds set in advance, then continue, pivot or stop.K3 · Level 4 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  3. Lesson 3. Initiative and bouncing backWrite a 90-day plan with fallbacks, anticipate obstacles, and turn each failure into a specific adjustment within 48 hours.K6 · Level 4 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  4. Lesson 4. The survival numbersBuild a 13-week cash flow forecast, work out gross margin and break-even, and know how many months you have before the cash runs out.K5 · Level 3 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  5. Lesson 5. Legal basics for starting upCompare business forms with four questions, know about recent support for small firms, and complete a checklist before you register.K10 · Level 3 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  6. Lesson 6. Hiring your first employeesDescribe the job as 90-day results, test with a real task, get the contract and insurance right, then keep people with weekly one-to-ones.K9 · Level 3 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  7. Lesson 7. The 3-minute pitchBuild a 5-part, 3-minute pitch on evidence from your experiments, separate actual from forecast figures, and end with a specific request.K2 · Level 3 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups
  8. Lesson 8. Digital operations and dataMixed review of the whole course, then a digital tools table, a data notice for customers and 3 AI rules for a small business.K10 · Level 3 · 20 min · Founders and start-ups

Note: Lessons 5, 6 and 8 are not legal advice, and Lesson 4 is not accounting or tax advice. Their legal examples come from Vietnam, with the date they were checked. Each lesson has a "Level of evidence" box. Also useful: Delegate and follow up (N5) once your team starts to grow.
