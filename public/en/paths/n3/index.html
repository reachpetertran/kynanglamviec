N3 New and junior employees

You have a job. Focus: getting through your first 90 days well, managing tasks and commitments, solving problems in a structured way, taking feedback and using AI safely.

For: People new to work, entry-level or junior staff.
Focus levels: Levels 2–3

Map of the Your First 90 Days course (N3)

The course has 7 lessons, taken in order. One thread runs through the course: your 90-day notebook, with 6 sheets you use every day at work. The last lesson turns them into a file for your review.

  1. Lesson 1. Prioritising competing tasksUse an impact × deadline matrix, and one clear question when two people give you urgent work.K5 · Level 3 · 20 min · New and junior employees
  2. Lesson 2. Confirming actions after meetingsTake meeting notes in 3 boxes, then send a summary within 24 hours so everyone agrees on the same actions.K1 · Level 3 · 15 min · New and junior employees
  3. Lesson 3. Serving customersFour steps (listen fully, confirm, offer an option with a time, follow up) for customers and colleagues alike.K3 · Level 3 · 20 min · New and junior employees
  4. Lesson 4. Finding the root causeAsk "Why?" several times, check each answer with data, then separate the quick fix from the root fix.K3 · Level 3 · 20 min · New and junior employees
  5. Lesson 5. When work goes off planFour steps after a mistake or a change of plan, and 4 questions to learn from it.K6 · Level 3 · 15 min · New and junior employees
  6. Lesson 6. Working with other teamsDraw a coordination map, hand over in 3 lines, and raise disagreements by focusing on the work.K8 · Level 3 · 20 min · New and junior employees
  7. Lesson 7. Review: your 90-day reviewGather evidence from your 90-day notebook, assess yourself, then set goals for the next 90 days.K7 · Level 3 · 20 min · New and junior employees

More to learn: the N3 lessons further down this page (90-day plan, progress reports, feedback, using AI safely, proposing improvements) add to the course. Coming later: a lesson on contracts, probation and workplace rules.
