About

kynanglamviec.com teaches work skills along a career path, from school pupils to managers. Free, public, no login needed.

How the site is built

  • 10 skills, 5 clusters, 5 levels, based on international frameworks and adapted to Vietnam.
  • Every lesson follows 9 parts, with a hypothetical workplace scenario.
  • Learning uses self-testing and spaced review, two techniques with strong evidence (Dunlosky and colleagues, 2013).
  • No real company or customer names are used. Every scenario is hypothetical.

The English edition

The site is written in Vietnamese first. English pages are adapted for international readers: names, places and examples are changed, and Vietnamese law appears only as an example with its source. The meaning and method stay the same. Lessons are added in stages, starting with the Getting Ready for Work course (N2).

Privacy

No login, no cookies, no trackers, no external scripts or fonts. Progress, self-assessment and journal entries are stored only in your browser (localStorage). The site has no server that stores user data.
